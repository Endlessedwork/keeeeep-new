# Privacy Policy for Keeeeep

**Last updated: August 2025**

## Introduction

Keeeeep ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, and password
- **Bookmark Data**: URLs, titles, descriptions, tags, and categories you save
- **User-Generated Content**: Notes and custom descriptions you add to bookmarks

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Website Metadata**: When you save a bookmark, we fetch publicly available information (title, description, images) from the URL
- **AI-Generated Summaries**: We use OpenAI's API to generate Thai language summaries of your bookmarks

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the Keeeeep service
- Generate AI summaries of bookmarked websites
- Organize and categorize your bookmarks
- Improve user experience

## Data Storage

**Cloud Storage**: Your account and bookmark data are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and your sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. Data is tied to your user account and is only accessible from that account.

**Local Storage**: Some data (such as sign-in state and app preferences) is kept on your device to make the app faster to use.

**AI Processing**: We use OpenAI's API to generate summaries. Website URLs and publicly available page content are sent to OpenAI for processing. Please review [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## Data Security

- Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot read your password
- Data in transit between the app and our services is encrypted with HTTPS
- Firestore security rules restrict access to your data to your own account
- No system is 100% secure, so we recommend a strong, unique password

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Firebase (Google)**: Authentication and cloud database for your bookmarks — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **OpenAI**: AI-powered content summarization
- Website metadata is fetched directly from the URLs you provide

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access and view all data you have saved in the app
- Edit or delete bookmarks and categories at any time
- Change your password from the Settings screen
- Request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us

## Children's Privacy

Keeeeep is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

To delete your data:
